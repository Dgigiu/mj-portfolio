## How I got here

I trained as a designer at Universidade de Aveiro and started out in print and web. In 2013 I joined Doist, where product design properly clicked for me. I worked on Todoist's visual scheduler and onboarding, and rebranded Doist itself. In 2014 I co-founded TOPDOX, a document collaboration tool that went through the Rockstart accelerator in Amsterdam, raised €1M in venture funding, and grew to a team of ~~around~~ twenty [the investment was aroung 1M, I'm not sure how many people we had at the peak, but I think 20 lands better than aroun 20 :)]. In 2018 I co-founded ikuTeam and led design on Team Files, a file management app for Jira and Confluence that reached around 10,000 companies before I left in 2025. Along the way I designed MyFoodways [this was also to help people eat better, more diverse diet, with more plants and less meat] and Food Save for Foodways, two apps focused on reducing food waste at home and in professional kitchens.

## Working with AI

Over the last couple of years, AI tools have become a real part of how I work. I use Claude, Perplexity, and Figma's AI features (including Make) for ideation, UX writing, early prototyping, and design critique. I'm not interested in AI as a novelty. I'm interested in shipping faster, with more consistent results, and using the time it gives back to think harder about the harder problems.

To stay sharp on what these tools can and can't do, I also build my own things. I have a working prototype of an app that aggregates my friends' BoardGameGeek collections and recommends the best games for whoever's around the table, built as a way to explore agentic AI on a problem my friend group actually has. ## Strategy and planning

A lot of my work has happened upstream of the screen. As a founder I helped shape product direction, roadmaps, and positioning. As the sole designer on small teams, I prioritized what to build next based on user feedback, usage data, and engineering reality. I've negotiated trade-offs with product managers and engineers, scoped features down to what could realistically ship, and made hard calls about where design effort would have the most leverage. Strategy and planning aren't separate from design for me. They're part of doing the job well.
