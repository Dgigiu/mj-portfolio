# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Design leads, hiring managers, and product people at SaaS and product companies. They skim on phones, read deeply on laptops, and decide quickly whether the work earns their time. Most arrive cold from a CV link or LinkedIn and give the site one chance.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Miguel Jesus, Senior Product Designer (20+ years, SaaS and mobile product design). The site exists to showcase product case studies (Team Files, MyFoodways, Food Save) and help land a senior in-house product design role. Success looks like a hiring manager reading one case study to the end and reaching out. Miguel is based in Porto and open to remote or hybrid roles only; copy never mentions relocation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, structured, confident. A product designer's product: the work carries the weight, the system stays out of the way. The discipline of tools like Linear or Vercel is a calibration for restraint and finish, not a look to borrow. This site speaks in its own register: warm paper instead of cool gray, a serif reading voice, one working blue. Voice is clear, grounded, US English. No marketing fluff, no superlatives, no em dashes.

## Anti-references

- Art director showcases: spectacle over substance, the system upstaging the work.
- Marketing-site flourishes and SaaS landing-page cliches.
- Generic AI design aesthetics: gradients for their own sake, overdesigned cards, glassmorphism, default Bootstrap blues.
- Tech-minimal mimicry: reading as a copy of Linear, Vercel, or any developer-tool site. Their restraint is the reference point; their aesthetic is not.
- Carousels, scroll-jacking, autoplaying or looping motion.

## Design Principles

1. **The work carries the weight.** The system frames the case studies; it never competes with them.
2. **The static state stays calm; engagement gets met with color.** Interaction is where the blue lives.
3. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** Hierarchy, rhythm, and personality come from type, not decoration.
4. **Motion is quiet and earned.** Transform and opacity only, under ~400ms or scroll-driven, text never animates.
5. **Respect the reader's time.** Plain precise language, fast pages, nothing between the reader and the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast throughout (audited; the accent was darkened to #155fe8 and tertiary text to #6f6f6f to pass on the warm canvas). All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and the site works fully without JavaScript. Visible focus rings, semantic landmarks, one h1 per page. The muted gray #8d8d8d is decorative only and never carries readable text.
